Privacy Policy

Tag Party. Last updated October 2, 2026.

The short version: Tag Party has no accounts and no chat. It does not ask for your name or email, does not read your Discord profile, and does not use cookies, ads or analytics. The game only passes live gameplay messages between the players in a room, and it does not keep them.

Who this covers

Tag Party is a free multiplayer game that you can play in a web browser and as a Discord Activity. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Tag Party.

What we collect

Information you give us: none. There are no accounts, sign-ins, profiles or chat in the game.

Your Discord account: when you play as a Discord Activity, the game does not ask for access to your Discord username, avatar, ID, friends or servers.

Gameplay data: while you are in a room, your device sends gameplay messages through our server to the other players in the same room. These include your character's position and movement, your chosen colour, power-up use, and the room code. This data is held in the server's memory only while the room is open. It is not saved to a database and is discarded when the room closes or the server restarts.

Technical data: like any online service, the servers that deliver the game handle technical details such as IP addresses and request information so that the game can load and run. Our hosting provider may keep standard operating logs for security and reliability. When you play inside Discord, Discord's proxy sits between you and our server and is designed to hide players' IP addresses from us.

Cookies and tracking

The game does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics, and it does not show ads.

How we use information

Only to run the game: to load it, to connect players in the same room, and to keep the service working and secure. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Other companies involved

  • Discord runs the platform where the Activity appears. Discord's own Privacy Policy covers what Discord collects about you.
  • Our hosting provider (currently Render) runs the game server and may keep technical logs as described above.
  • GitHub stores the game's source code. It does not receive your gameplay data.

Children

Tag Party is not directed at children under 13. To play it through Discord you must meet Discord's minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because we do not keep personal data about you, there is normally nothing to delete or export. If you have a question or a privacy request, contact us using the email below and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date.
